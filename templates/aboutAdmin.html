About SalesCon

Welcome to SalesCon! We are your all-in-one platform designed to streamline vendor management and event organization on the Universiti Sains Malaysia campus. Whether you're a vendor, organizer, or attendee, SalesCon offers a seamless experience for everyone involved in campus events.

Our Mission

To create a collaborative platform that simplifies vendor registration, slot booking, and payment processes, while enhancing compliance and analytics for better decision-making.

Core Features

  • Centralized vendor management system
  • Seamless slot booking process
  • Hygiene and compliance tracking
  • Integrated payment system
  • Event analytics for better decision-making

Why Choose SalesCon?

With SalesCon, we aim to revolutionize how campus events are organized, making them more efficient, transparent, and enjoyable for everyone.
